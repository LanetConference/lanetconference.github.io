About the Conference

Advancing Network Science Across Latin America

LANET is the premier forum in Latin America for the exchange of ideas and results in complex networks — spanning physics, biology, computer science, social sciences, and beyond.

The 5th Latin American Conference on Complex Networks (LANET 2027) brings together researchers, practitioners, and students from across Latin America and the world to advance our collective understanding of complex systems and network science.

LANET 2027 will be held at the Centro Cultural UFRGS (Rua Eng. Luiz Englert, s/n, Porto Alegre, RS) — a distinguished historic venue at the heart of the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul campus in southern Brazil.
