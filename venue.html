Venue & Travel

Porto Alegre, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil

Host Institution

Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul

One of Brazil's most prestigious public research universities, consistently ranked among the top institutions in Latin America.

Founded in 1895, UFRGS has a strong tradition in complex systems research and network science, making it an ideal host for the 5th edition of LANET.

LANET 2027 will be held at the Centro Cultural UFRGS, a striking historic building in the heart of Porto Alegre's city centre, just steps from the main UFRGS campus.

Destination

About Porto Alegre

A vibrant, cosmopolitan city in southern Brazil with a rich cultural and academic heritage.

The City

Porto Alegre is the capital of Rio Grande do Sul, home to over 1.4 million people. Known for its European heritage, gaúcho culture, strong university scene, and lively arts and food culture.

Climate in July

Late July is the middle of winter in Porto Alegre. Temperatures typically range between 10 °C and 18 °C. Light layers and a jacket are recommended.

Getting There

Porto Alegre is served by Salgado Filho International Airport (POA), with direct connections to major Brazilian cities and international hubs. The airport is approximately 8 km from Centro Cultural UFRGS.

Accommodation

A range of hotels and guesthouses are available near the UFRGS campus and city centre. A list of recommended options will be published closer to the event.

Local Transport

Porto Alegre has an extensive bus network and metro line. Ride-hailing apps operate widely and are a convenient option for getting around the city.

Food & Culture

Porto Alegre is famous for churrasco, chimarrão, and a lively restaurant scene. The city centre and Moinhos de Vento neighbourhood offer excellent dining options.

Entry Requirements

Visa Information

Brazil's entry requirements vary by nationality. Please check well in advance of travel.

We recommend verifying current visa requirements with your national embassy or the Brazilian consulate in your country. Visa processing times can be lengthy — please plan accordingly.

Many nationalities may enter Brazil visa-free for tourism and academic visits. However, requirements vary and are subject to change.

If you require a formal letter of invitation for visa purposes, please contact the organising committee once your abstract has been accepted.
