Conference Programme

The full programme will be published following the review process. The outline below illustrates the expected conference structure.
Keynote Talks
Twenty-two keynote speakers, 30 minutes each, opening every morning and afternoon in plenary
Contributed Talks
Fourteen themed sessions of selected oral presentations, running in two parallel tracks
Lightning Talks
Short presentations introducing accepted work, one on each of days 2 to 5
Poster Sessions
Interactive poster presentations closing days 2 and 4
Schedule

Programme at a Glance

Keynote talks open each morning and afternoon in plenary, with contributed talks following in two parallel tracks. Exact timings and session details will be confirmed once the review process is complete.

Rooms are still to be confirmed. “Plenary Room” is the single room used for keynote talks, the opening and closing ceremonies and the lightning talk sessions; “Room 1” and “Room 2” are the two parallel tracks used for contributed talks. Actual room names will be published closer to the event.
